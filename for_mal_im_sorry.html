I'm sorry, MAL

A little message from someone who wishes he could be there beside you, not just through a screen.

♡ ✿ ♡

First, I'm sorry, mahal.

I’m sorry that whenever I try to surprise you with a gift, especially when it’s your day off, the delivery somehow gets delayed or doesn’t arrive on time.

I know you might be looking forward to it, and I know it can be disappointing when you set aside time or hope it’ll arrive that day, only for it not to come. I’m sorry if I made you feel let down, even though all I wanted was to make you smile.

I wish I could control the delivery, but I know that saying that doesn’t take away the disappointment. I just want you to know that I understand why it hurts, and I’m sorry, MAL. 🤍

I never want my gifts to become something you have to wait sadly for. They’re supposed to be little reminders that you’re loved, appreciated, and always on my mind.

It’s not because I don’t try.

Every time I order something for you, I imagine your reaction and how happy I hope it’ll make you. I get excited too, because even from far away, I want to find little ways to make you feel special.

But I also know that my intentions don’t erase how you feel when the package arrives late. I don’t want to make excuses or ask you to pretend you aren’t disappointed. Your feelings matter to me, mahal.

Even if a parcel is late, my love for you isn’t. ♡

One day, I’ll bring it to you myself.

This distance won’t be our whole story forever, MAL. I keep looking forward to the day when I won’t have to track a parcel and hope it reaches you on time.

Someday, I want to be the one standing in front of you, holding your flowers and your gifts in my own hands. I want to see your face when I give them to you, hear your laugh right there beside me, and hug you instead of wishing I could through a phone.

My promise to you 🌿

I’ll keep doing my best to plan ahead, choose gifts with care, and make our special days meaningful. And when we finally get to close this distance, I want to deliver those little surprises personally—not through a courier, but with my own two hands and a hug that’s been waiting for you for so long.

🌸

Can I tell you one more thing?

Tap the little button, mahal.

You are more important to me than any gift I could buy. The gifts are just my small way of reaching across the distance, but what I really want is for you to feel loved by me—even on days when things don’t go as planned. Thank you for being patient with me, mahal. I’m sorry for the times I’ve disappointed you, and I’ll keep learning how to love you better. Until I can give you these things in person, please remember that my heart is already there with you. 🤍

I love you, MAL. Always trying, always learning. ♡
